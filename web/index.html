Built for freelancers who get paid from abroad.

The problem

Great work shouldn't be hard to get paid for.

Many freelancers can't use the platforms their clients pay with. The workarounds are slow, expensive and built on trust in a middleman. Stablecoins fix the money. Seed phrases, gas tokens and wrong-network mistakes still keep most people out.

How it works

Three steps. No crypto knowledge needed.

  1. 01

    Sign up with a passkey

    Your wallet is created with Face ID or your fingerprint. There's nothing to write down and nothing to lose.

  2. 02

    Send an invoice link

    Set the amount and description. Share the link by email, chat or anywhere your client is.

  3. 03

    Get paid in USDC

    Your client pays from any wallet. The money goes straight to you, and the invoice shows paid.

Why SeedlessPay

Built to be trusted with your money.

No seed phrase

Avalanche verifies passkey signatures natively, so your wallet is secured by your device, not 24 words on paper.

We never hold your funds

Payments move directly from your client to you in a single transfer. The contract has no balance to lose.

No owner, no backdoor

The contract can't be paused, changed or redirected after deployment. Anyone can read the source.

Paid exactly once

Each invoice can only be settled once, and nobody can block it by paying first with different terms.
