How it works

Getting paid takes three simple steps.

  1. 01

    Create your account

    Sign up with Face ID or your fingerprint. Your wallet is created for you, and there is no password or recovery phrase to keep safe.

  2. 02

    Send a payment link

    Enter the amount and a short description of the work, then share the link with your client by email or chat.

  3. 03

    Receive your money

    Your client pays in USDC from their own wallet. The money reaches you in a few seconds, and the invoice is marked as paid.

Security

Your money stays under your control.

We never hold your money

Payments go directly from your client's wallet to yours. SeedlessPay does not keep your funds at any point.

There is nothing to write down

Your phone works as the key to your wallet, so there is no seed phrase that could be lost or stolen.

Each invoice is paid only once

When an invoice is paid, it cannot be paid again by mistake, and nobody else can block it.

Our code is public

Questions

Common questions

What is USDC?

USDC is a digital dollar issued by Circle. One USDC is designed to always be worth one US dollar, so the amount on your invoice keeps its value.

Does my client need a SeedlessPay account?

No. Your client can pay the link from any wallet that holds USDC on Avalanche. Only you need an account to send invoices.

How do I turn USDC into my local money?

You can sell USDC for your local currency through a crypto exchange or a peer-to-peer (P2P) service that works in your country, and receive the money in your bank account or mobile wallet. Some services only accept USDT, and in that case you can swap your USDC to USDT first. SeedlessPay does not run these services, so please check their fees and the rules where you live.

How does my client get USDC on Avalanche?

Most exchanges let you withdraw USDC to the Avalanche network. Your client should choose USDC and the network called "Avalanche C-Chain" (sometimes shown as AVAXC). Other networks, such as the Avalanche X-Chain or Ethereum, will not work with SeedlessPay. Your client also needs a very small amount of AVAX in their wallet to pay the network fee.

How does paying in milestones with escrow work?

For bigger jobs or new clients, you can split an invoice into up to 5 milestones. Your client pays the full amount into escrow first, so you can see the money is really there before you start. The money is held by a public contract with no owner. Neither you, your client, nor SeedlessPay can take it outside the rules.

When you deliver a milestone, your client has 7 days to approve it or raise an issue. If they say nothing, you can claim the money. If you miss a due date, your client can take that milestone back. If an issue is still not resolved after 30 days, the money goes back to the client, unless you both agree on a split first.

Escrow protects you from a client who cannot pay or goes silent. It cannot judge the quality of your work. Until a milestone is approved, share previews or watermarked files rather than the final work.

What if I lose my phone?

Your wallet is protected by a passkey. If your passkey is saved in a password manager like iCloud Keychain or Google Password Manager, it is synced to your other devices, and you can sign in on a new phone or computer and see the same wallet, money and history. If your passkey was saved only on the lost device and was never synced, the wallet cannot be opened, and we cannot recover it for you. We recommend keeping passkey sync turned on.

Who holds the money after my client pays?

You do. The payment moves from your client's wallet directly to your wallet in one step, and SeedlessPay never has access to it.

Which countries can use SeedlessPay?

SeedlessPay is built for freelancers and clients anywhere in the world. You can use it wherever using stablecoins is legal for you. Please check the rules in your country, including how you should report income for taxes.
