Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains what information SeedlessPay collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Who we are

SeedlessPay is run by Sithu Nyein, an individual based in Myanmar. SeedlessPay is not yet a registered company. If you have any question about your privacy, email us at hello@seedlesspay.com.

What this website collects

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and you do not need an account to use it. The only information you can give us is your email address, if you join the waitlist or email us.

  • Theme choice. If you switch between the light and dark theme, your choice is saved in your own browser so the site remembers it. It is never sent to us.
  • Fonts and images. All fonts and images are served from our own website, so no third-party font or image service sees your visit.

Hosting

The website is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel processes basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, so it can deliver the site and protect it from abuse. You can read how Vercel handles this data in the Vercel Privacy Policy.

When you join the waitlist

If you join the waitlist, we store your email address and the date you joined. We use it only to invite you to SeedlessPay when your spot is ready and to send you important updates about the beta. We do not sell, rent or share it with anyone for marketing.

Waitlist emails are stored in a database provided by Upstash, connected through our host, Vercel. To stop spam, we also keep a one-way scrambled version of your IP address for one hour, which cannot be turned back into your IP address. It is deleted automatically after that hour.

You can ask us to remove your email from the waitlist at any time by emailing hello@seedlesspay.com.

When you email us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and your message. We use this only to reply to you. Our email is forwarded through ImprovMX and stored with Google (Gmail).

We do not sell, rent or share your email address with anyone for marketing. You can ask us to delete your messages and email address at any time.

Payments on the blockchain

SeedlessPay payments are recorded on the Avalanche blockchain. Blockchain records are public and permanent, which means anyone can see wallet addresses, amounts and the time of each payment. We cannot change or delete information that is recorded on a blockchain.

When the app launches

The SeedlessPay app is not yet open to the public. If it collects more information when it launches, such as account or invoice details, we will update this policy before that happens and explain what is collected and why.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Email hello@seedlesspay.com and we will reply within 30 days.

Children

SeedlessPay is not intended for anyone under 18 years old, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
