# Code of Conduct

SeedlessPay is built for freelancers and clients all over the world. We want this project and its community to be a welcoming place for everyone, whatever their country, background, experience or the language they speak best.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being kind and patient, especially with people who are new to crypto or to open source
- Giving and accepting feedback with respect
- Writing in clear, simple language so that people who speak English as a second language can follow
- Focusing on what is best for the people who use SeedlessPay
- Owning mistakes and learning from them

Examples of behavior that is not acceptable:

- Insults, harassment, threats or personal attacks
- Discriminatory jokes or language about any person or group
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwanted attention of any kind
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission
- Spam, scams, phishing links, or asking anyone for seed phrases, private keys or passkeys
- Promoting tokens, investments or other projects without permission

## Scope

This code applies in all SeedlessPay spaces, including GitHub issues and pull requests, the Telegram community, X and LinkedIn replies, and any event where someone represents SeedlessPay.

## Enforcement

If you see or experience behavior that breaks this code, please report it to [hello@seedlesspay.com](mailto:hello@seedlesspay.com) with the subject line `Conduct report`. Reports are handled privately.

Maintainers may remove messages, comments, code or other contributions that break this code, and may warn, mute or ban anyone involved, depending on how serious the behavior is. Scams and requests for keys or seed phrases lead to an immediate ban.
